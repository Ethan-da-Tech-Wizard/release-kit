# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome people with different backgrounds and experience, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, take responsibility for your mistakes, and focus on what is best for the project. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information, and sexualized language or imagery are not acceptable.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban a person for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

To report a problem, email **ethan@twu.software**. Reports are handled privately and in good faith. The full text of the Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines, is at the link above and applies here.
